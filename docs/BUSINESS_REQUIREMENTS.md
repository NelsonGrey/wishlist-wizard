## 📋 Executive Summary

**What**: Wishlist Wizard is a comprehensive multi-platform wishlist management application enabling users to create, share, collaborate, and monetize wishlists through affiliate partnerships with e-commerce platforms.

**Why**: The global e-gift market is $250B+ and growing 15% annually. Gift-givers struggle with:
- Decision paralysis when selecting gifts (average 2-3 hours per gift search)
- Gift duplicate risk ("I already got them a scarf")
- Disconnected gift tracking across family/social groups
- Missing price-drop opportunities on gifts they were considering
- No monetization for wishlist creators or curators

**Market Opportunity**: Target the $250B+ e-gift market + $150B+ social gifting space with a digital-first solution that adds intelligence (price tracking, recommendations, group coordination) and monetization (affiliate revenue sharing).

**Business Model**: 
1. **Primary**: Affiliate commissions (8-15%) on items purchased through Wishlist Wizard links
2. **Secondary**: Premium features subscription ($99/year) for advanced analytics, group gifting
3. **Tertiary**: B2B (influencers, content creators, brands) partnerships

**Current Status**: ✅ MVP complete with Web, Mobile, Browser Extension. 100% core features implemented. Ready for growth phase and affiliate monetization.

### Unit Economics

#### Cost Structure (per paid user, monthly)
- **COGS**:
  - Infrastructure (API calls, database, storage): $0.80
  - Payment processing (Stripe, etc.): $0.40
  - Affiliate tracking/verification: $0.20
  - Support (1% conversion to paid): $0.10
  - **Total COGS: $1.50/user**

- **CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)**:
  - Organic/viral: $0 (majority of free users)
  - Paid ads (Facebook, YouTube): $2/user
  - Referral incentives ($5 credit): $1/user for referred
  - **Blended CAC: $0.40/user for free tier, $2/user for premium**

#### Profitability Path
- **Free Users**: Supported by affiliate revenue (per-transaction), NPS building
- **Premium Users**: ARPU $99/year = **$8.25/month**
  - COGS: $1.50/month
  - CAC payback: $2 / month ÷ ($8.25 - $1.50) = **0.35 months** (excellent)
  - LTV: $8.25 × 24 month avg = **$198** per premium user
  - LTV:CAC ratio: $198 / $2 = **99:1** (exceptional unit economics)

#### Gross Margin
- **Free tier**: 100% margin from affiliate revenue
- **Premium tier**: ~80% gross margin (revenue - COGS)
- **Blended gross margin Year 1**: ~70% (mix of free affiliate revenue + premium subscriptions)

## 📌 Key Decisions & Trade-offs

### Decision 1: Affiliate-First vs. Subscription-First Model
**Decision**: Affiliate-first (70% revenue) + subscription secondary (20%)
- **Rationale**: Lower barrier to user adoption (free with affiliate revenue), aligns with creator monetization, immediate revenue generation
- **Trade-off**: Margin dependent on partner commission rates (less stable than pure SaaS)

### Decision 2: Creator Economy Focus
**Decision**: Heavy emphasis on creator/influencer monetization (50% of MAU target)
- **Rationale**: Creators drive distribution, high LTV, network effects (creators attract followers to platform)
- **Trade-off**: Requires support infrastructure, fraud prevention, high engagement to keep creators

### Decision 3: Multi-Platform from Day One
**Decision**: Web + iOS + Android + Browser Extension at launch/near-launch
- **Rationale**: Users expect seamless mobile + web experience, browser extension captures purchase moment
- **Trade-off**: Higher engineering complexity, longer time to market

### Decision 4: Direct E-Commerce Integration
**Decision**: Deep integrations with Amazon, Etsy, Walmart, etc. (not affiliate-only links)
- **Rationale**: Enables features (price tracking, inventory sync, reviews) and better UX
- **Trade-off**: API dependencies, maintenance burden, potential policy changes
