Wishlist Wizard makes it simple to build, share, and manage wishlists for every occasion — birthdays, holidays, weddings, baby showers, or just things you want.

BUILD YOUR WISHLIST
Add items by snapping a photo or pasting a product link. Organize everything into separate wishlists for different occasions or people.

SHARE AND COLLABORATE
Share a wishlist with friends and family so they always know what to get. Collaborate on group wishlists together.

TRACK PRICES
Turn on price tracking for items you're watching and get notified when the price drops, so you never overpay.

SIGN IN AND SYNC
Your wishlists sync across devices, so you can add items on the go and check them from anywhere.

FREE TO START
Create up to 3 wishlists free. Upgrade to Starter, Plus, Creator, or Business for more wishlists, unlimited items, group gifting, analytics, and more.

Wishlist Wizard is built for gift-givers and gift-getters alike — stop guessing what to buy, and stop hoping people remember what you actually want.
